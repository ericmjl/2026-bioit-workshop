# Facilitator Runbook — EARS

**Parent LLD**: [Workshop Operations LLD](./LLD.md)  
**Artifact**: `resources/facilitator-runbook.md`

## Runbook Content

- [ ] **ART-RUNBOOK-001**: The runbook shall provide an outline organized by the three workshop parts (plus break between Parts 1 and 2).
- [ ] **ART-RUNBOOK-002**: The runbook shall include default lead/support assignments and a brief co-presenter support playbook.
- [ ] **ART-RUNBOOK-003**: Each part shall list facilitator **Say** scripts, **Show**/demo steps, and timeboxing guidance where the LLD specifies priorities (e.g., do not cut keystone or exit reflection).
- [ ] **ART-RUNBOOK-004**: The runbook shall include Part 1 discussion blocks, both live demos, and bridge-out to break.
- [ ] **ART-RUNBOOK-005**: The runbook shall include Part 2 discussion, mini-lecture bullets, formality aside, keystone exercise, and bridge to Part 3.
- [ ] **ART-RUNBOOK-006**: The runbook shall include Part 3 bridge-in, both agent demos (with fallback paths), exit reflection, and close.
- [ ] **ART-RUNBOOK-007**: The runbook shall include a fallback cheat sheet and a condensed presenter prep checklist.
