# Part 2 Keystone Exercise — EARS

**Parent LLD**: [Part 2 LLD](./LLD.md)

## Exercise Structure

- [ ] **P2-KEY-001**: The keystone exercise shall run for approximately 8 minutes total.
- [ ] **P2-KEY-002**: The individual phase shall allow 3 minutes for attendees to write one change.
- [ ] **P2-KEY-003**: The pair-share phase shall allow 3 minutes for neighbors to compare keystone choices.
- [ ] **P2-KEY-004**: Where time permits, the lead shall invite one optional plenary volunteer (≤2 min).

## Individual Prompt

- [ ] **P2-KEY-010**: The lead shall instruct attendees to write one change that is **high pain relief, low effort**.
- [ ] **P2-KEY-011**: The prompt shall include examples: standardize artifact type, adopt repo template, migrate off conda, require docs in repo.

## Anchor Stories

- [ ] **P2-KEY-020**: Where the room is quiet during pair share, the lead may offer the pixi migration story (conda pain → staged migration → unlocked research).
- [ ] **P2-KEY-021**: Where the room is quiet, the lead may offer the standardizing shipping format story (clarity for collaborators).

## Monday Conversation Script

- [ ] **P2-KEY-030**: Each attendee shall leave Part 2 with a rough script for their team conversation about standardizing what they ship.
- [ ] **P2-KEY-031**: The script shall reference attending a workshop on DS team practices and proposing one or two technical artifacts the team always ships.

## Attendee Output

- [ ] **P2-KEY-040**: Each attendee shall have one keystone change written down before Part 3 begins.
- [ ] **P2-KEY-041**: The keystone change shall be referenced again in Part 3's bridge-in (P3-BRG-001).

## Verification (Dry Run)

- [ ] **P2-KEY-050**: During dry run, the keystone exercise shall not be cut even if earlier segments overrun.
