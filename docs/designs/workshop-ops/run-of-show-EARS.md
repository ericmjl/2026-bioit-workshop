# Workshop Run of Show — EARS

**Parent LLD**: [Workshop Operations LLD](./LLD.md)

## Master Timing

- [ ] **OPS-ROS-001**: Part 1 (Foundations and Tooling) shall run for 45 minutes.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-002**: The break shall run for 15 minutes immediately after Part 1.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-003**: Part 2 (Software Standardization) shall run for 45 minutes.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-004**: Part 3 (Agents and Automation) shall run for 45 minutes.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-005**: Total delivered content shall be approximately 2 hours 15 minutes within a 3-hour booked slot.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-006**: Where time remains, presenters may use up to 15 minutes of Q&A buffer.

## Co-Presenter Roles

- [ ] **OPS-ROS-010**: Default assignment shall be documented before dry run: Part 1 Eric leads / Jackie supports; Part 2 Jackie leads / Eric supports; Part 3 Eric leads / Jackie supports.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-011**: Lead and support roles shall swap at the break unless presenters agree otherwise.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-012**: When supporting, the co-presenter shall triage audience questions before routing to the mic.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-013**: When supporting during demos, the co-presenter shall watch for errors and have fallback recordings ready.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-014**: Either presenter may contribute popcorn-style examples without competing with the lead's voice.

## Hard Stops

- [ ] **OPS-ROS-020**: If Part 1 discussion overruns at 28 minutes, the lead shall cut plenary to one share and proceed to demos.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-021**: If Part 2 overruns, the lead shall cut optional plenary but shall not cut the keystone exercise.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-022**: If Part 3 overruns, the lead shall shorten Marimo Pair before cutting exit reflection.

## Dry-Run Verification

- [ ] **OPS-ROS-030**: Dry run shall confirm Part 1 completes in ≤45 min with at least one plenary share.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-031**: Dry run shall confirm Part 2 keystone exercise is not cut.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-032**: Dry run shall confirm Part 3 demos work or fallbacks are ready.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-033**: Dry run shall confirm role swap at break feels smooth.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-034**: Dry run shall confirm total content ≤2h 15m.

## Room and AV

- [ ] **OPS-ROS-040**: Terminal font size shall be ≥16pt and readable from the back row.
- [ ] **OPS-ROS-041**: A microphone or support question-repeating protocol shall be available for audience questions.
