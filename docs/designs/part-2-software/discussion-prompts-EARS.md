# Part 2 Discussion Prompts — EARS

**Parent LLD**: [Part 2 LLD](./LLD.md)

## Bridge In

- [ ] **P2-BRG-001**: When Part 2 begins, the lead shall deliver the bridge-in script: Part 1 was the destination; Part 2 is the road (practices + one change first).
- [ ] **P2-BRG-002**: The bridge-in shall complete within 5 minutes.

## Block A: Docs, Refactor, Test Experience

- [ ] **P2-DISC-001**: Block A shall include the prompt: "Does your team write docs? Where do they live — repo, wiki, nowhere?"
- [ ] **P2-DISC-002**: Block A shall include the prompt: "When do you refactor vs ship and move on?"
- [ ] **P2-DISC-003**: Block A shall include the prompt: "What testing exists today — none, manual, automated, CI?"
- [ ] **P2-DISC-004**: Block A shall include the prompt: "What would your team push back on if you introduced any of these?"
- [ ] **P2-DISC-005**: The lead shall connect Block A to Part 1: "Given what your team ships today, which of these is the biggest gap?"
- [ ] **P2-DISC-006**: Block A shall run for approximately 10 minutes.
- [ ] **P2-DISC-007**: Before the mini-lecture, the lead shall deliver the bridge: "You don't need perfection — enough structure that the next person, or an agent, can follow."

## Mini-Lecture: Doc, Refactor, Test

- [ ] **P2-LECT-001**: The mini-lecture shall cover documentation (what the next person needs: install, run, inputs/outputs, assumptions).
- [ ] **P2-LECT-002**: The mini-lecture shall cover refactoring (signal = duplicated code; destination = modules, CLI, package).
- [ ] **P2-LECT-003**: The mini-lecture shall cover testing (smoke tests on core logic; CI on push; trust for refactors).
- [ ] **P2-LECT-004**: The mini-lecture shall differentiate from Part 1: Part 1 showed doc publishing; Part 2 covers what to write and when to start.
- [ ] **P2-LECT-005**: The mini-lecture shall complete within 10 minutes.

## Formality Aside

- [ ] **P2-LECT-010**: The formality aside shall state: regulated contexts → buy validated tools.
- [ ] **P2-LECT-011**: The formality aside shall state: research/exploratory → build for yourself.
- [ ] **P2-LECT-012**: The formality aside shall state: shared internal platform → build with docs and tests.
- [ ] **P2-LECT-013**: The formality aside shall complete within 2 minutes and shall not expand into a standalone build-vs-buy debate.

## Bridge Out

- [ ] **P2-BRG-010**: Before Part 3, the lead shall deliver the bridge-out script: named one practice → Part 3 covers agents that compress the cost.

## Edge Cases

- [ ] **P2-EDGE-001**: If an attendee says testing is overkill for notebooks, the lead shall agree for one-offs and draw the line at "reused more than twice."
- [ ] **P2-EDGE-002**: If Part 1 ran long, the lead shall cut optional plenary share but shall not cut the keystone exercise.
