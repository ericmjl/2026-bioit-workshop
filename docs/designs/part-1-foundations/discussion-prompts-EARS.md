# Part 1 Discussion Prompts — EARS

**Parent LLD**: [Part 1 LLD](./LLD.md)

## Framing

- [ ] **P1-FRM-001**: The lead shall open Part 1 with a framing monologue (≤5 min) stating the audience is data science team leads and the session goal is to inventory what their team ships and see what good could look like.
- [ ] **P1-FRM-002**: The lead shall instruct attendees to discuss their **team's** work, not personal side projects.

## Block A: Team Work Inventory

- [ ] **P1-INV-001**: When Block A begins, the lead shall assign attendees to neighbor groups of 3–4.
- [ ] **P1-INV-002**: The lead shall read the Block A setup script aloud before groups begin.
- [ ] **P1-INV-003**: Block A shall include the prompt: "What does your team produce in a typical month? List concrete outputs."
- [ ] **P1-INV-004**: Block A shall include the prompt: "Are those outputs consistent across team members, or does everyone ship something different?"
- [ ] **P1-INV-005**: Block A shall include the prompt: "If a collaborator asked 'what does your data science team deliver?', could every member give the same answer?"
- [ ] **P1-INV-006**: Block A shall include the prompt: "What fraction of work feels like a one-off analysis vs. something reused?"
- [ ] **P1-INV-007**: Block A shall run for approximately 15 minutes.
- [ ] **P1-INV-008**: If Block A overruns, the lead shall not skip Block B — cut plenary shares instead.

## Block B: Bus Factor and Onboarding

- [ ] **P1-BUS-001**: Block B shall include the prompt: "If your most specialized team member left tomorrow, how long until someone else could continue their work?"
- [ ] **P1-BUS-002**: Block B shall include the prompt: "How easy is it for team members to onboard onto each other's repos/notebooks?"
- [ ] **P1-BUS-003**: Block B shall include the prompt: "Do you see silos — individuals working in bespoke ways that others can't follow?"
- [ ] **P1-BUS-004**: Block B shall include the prompt: "Is your team growing? Does that change the urgency?"
- [ ] **P1-BUS-005**: Block B shall run for approximately 10 minutes.

## Plenary Debrief

- [ ] **P1-PLN-001**: When discussion blocks complete, the lead shall facilitate a plenary debrief of 2–3 group shares (≤5 min).
- [ ] **P1-PLN-002**: If discussion ran long, the lead shall cut plenary to one share and proceed to demos.

## Bridge to Demos

- [ ] **P1-BRG-001**: Before demos begin, the lead shall deliver the bridge script: gaps surfaced (inconsistency, bus factor, onboarding) → "this is the destination, not the tutorial."

## Edge Cases

- [ ] **P1-EDGE-001**: If an attendee says their team has no problems, the lead shall validate and ask: "What would break first if you doubled in size?"
- [ ] **P1-EDGE-002**: If discussion energy is low, the lead shall use the dashboard provocation: "How many of you ship dashboards? How many maintain them?"
