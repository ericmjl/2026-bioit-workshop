# Part 3 Demo Scripts — EARS

**Parent LLD**: [Part 3 LLD](./LLD.md)

## Bridge In and Mini-Lecture

- [ ] **P3-BRG-001**: When Part 3 begins, the lead shall reference the keystone change attendees wrote in Part 2.
- [ ] **P3-BRG-002**: The lead shall state that agents amplify standards; they do not replace them.
- [ ] **P3-BRG-003**: The bridge-in and mini-lecture shall complete within 8 minutes.
- [ ] **P3-BRG-004**: Where useful, the support presenter shall run a 1-minute pulse check: who has access to a coding agent at work?

## Demo 1: Architecture Improvement on Messy Notebooks

- [ ] **P3-DMO-010**: Demo 1 shall open `examples/messy/` with 2–3 Marimo notebooks showing duplicated, slightly modified functions.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-011**: The lead shall frame messy notebooks as the analysis treadmill from Part 1 and absent Part 2 practices.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-012**: The lead shall explain "seams" and "deepen" (≤30 sec) and point to the resource in the repo.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-013**: The lead shall prompt a coding agent with Matt Pocock's codebase architecture improvement skill against the notebook collection.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-014**: Demo 1 shall show `examples/clean/` as the refactored output (live or pre-built fallback).
- [ ] **P3-DMO-015**: The lead shall tie back to Part 2: "Refactor when you duplicate — here's an agent doing that work."
- [ ] **P3-DMO-016**: Demo 1 shall complete within 18 minutes.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-017**: If the live agent is slow or fails, the lead shall cut to pre-built clean output and narrate the intended refactor.

## Demo 2: Marimo Pair

- [ ] **P3-DMO-020**: Demo 2 shall show Marimo Pair controlling a notebook in real time.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-021**: Demo 2 shall demonstrate at least two natural-language actions (e.g., add cell, refactor, explain).
- [ ] **P3-DMO-022**: The lead shall contrast in-notebook agent control with copy-paste into an external chat tool.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-023**: The lead shall point attendees to `resources/marimo-pair-setup.md` for optional follow-along.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-024**: Demo 2 shall complete within 10 minutes.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-025**: If running over time, the lead may cut Marimo Pair to 5 minutes or use a screen recording.

## Demo Tone

- [ ] **P3-DMO-030**: Part 3 demos shall be observe-along; hands-on follow-along is optional via the materials repo.

## Prep Verification (Dry Run)

- [ ] **P3-DMO-040**: Messy notebooks shall contain realistic duplication (not toy one-liners).
- [ ] **P3-DMO-041**: Pre-run agent refactor shall exist in `examples/clean/` before the workshop.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-042**: Architecture improvement skill shall be installed and tested on presenter's machine.
- [ ] **P3-DMO-043**: Marimo Pair shall be tested on presenter's machine with a pre-loaded notebook.
