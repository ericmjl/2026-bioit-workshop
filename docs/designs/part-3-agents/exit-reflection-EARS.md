# Part 3 Exit Reflection — EARS

**Parent LLD**: [Part 3 LLD](./LLD.md)

## Exit Reflection

- [ ] **P3-EXIT-001**: The exit reflection shall run for approximately 5 minutes.
- [ ] **P3-EXIT-002**: The lead shall instruct attendees to record three items individually (paper or notes app).
- [ ] **P3-EXIT-003**: Item 1 shall be: one technical artifact their team should standardize on.
- [ ] **P3-EXIT-004**: Item 2 shall be: one keystone change they will propose next week (from Part 2).
- [ ] **P3-EXIT-005**: Item 3 shall be: one tool they will explore post-workshop.

## Close

- [ ] **P3-EXIT-010**: The lead shall share the materials repo URL before dismissing attendees.
- [ ] **P3-EXIT-011**: The lead shall share the Data Science Bootstrap ebook link for depth.
- [ ] **P3-EXIT-012**: The lead shall close with: "If nothing is broken on your team, don't fix it. If clarity is missing, start with what you ship."
- [ ] **P3-EXIT-013**: Where time permits, the lead may use remaining buffer (0–15 min) for Q&A.

## Workshop Success Criteria (HLD Link)

- [ ] **P3-EXIT-020**: At workshop end, attendees shall be able to articulate one technical artifact, one keystone change, and one tool to explore.
- [ ] **P3-EXIT-021**: At workshop end, attendees shall be able to explain why standardization helps agents (not replaces them).

## Verification (Dry Run)

- [ ] **P3-EXIT-030**: During dry run, exit reflection shall fit within 5 minutes even if demos were shortened.
